## English

**Subtitle:** Manage your restaurant

**Keywords:** restaurant,cafe,menu,booking,reservations,reviews,offers,events,chef,owner,Iraq,Baghdad

**Promotional text:** Keep your menu, photos, opening hours and events up to date, and follow your bookings and reviews in one app.

**Description:**

LiGETA Partner is the app for restaurant and café owners and staff in Iraq. Manage how your restaurant appears on LiGETA, in Arabic and English.

• Menu: add dishes, prices and photos
• Profile: location, opening hours and contact details
• Gallery: share photos of your food and your place
• Offers: create promotions with start and end dates
• Events and chefs: present your chefs and publish special evenings
• Bookings: confirm or decline event bookings
• Reviews: read customer reviews and reply to them
• Staff: give your team their own sign-in

Create your account, set up your restaurant and send it for review. LiGETA checks every restaurant before it is shown to customers.

## App Review notes

This app is for restaurant owners and staff. Sign in with the demo account; no verification code is needed. Accounts are free and nothing is sold in the app. Account deletion: Settings & Staff > Delete Account. The camera is used only to scan a customer's visit QR code.
