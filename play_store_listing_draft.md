## Full description (max 4000 characters)

LiGETA Restaurant Partner is the all-in-one management app for restaurant
owners and staff in Iraq. Run your restaurant's public presence and daily
operations from your phone — no laptop required.

**Your menu, always up to date**
Add, edit, and price your dishes in seconds. Organize by category, mark your
most-ordered items, and run price updates across your whole menu at once.

**Real customer reviews, verified by QR code**
Every table gets a unique QR code customers scan to leave a real review —
rating, comments, and up to 4 photos. Reply publicly to any review, or get
an AI-drafted reply you can edit before sending.

**Photos that sell your restaurant**
Build a gallery across Food, Ambience, and Menu albums. Set a cover photo for
each album so your best shot is the first thing customers see.

**Promotions you control**
Create discounts — percentage or fixed amount — for your whole menu or
specific dishes. Set them one-time or recurring on a weekly schedule. Go
live immediately, no approval wait.

**Chef's Table & Events, with real booking management**
List special events — buffet nights, live music, tasting menus — and accept
reservation requests directly. See your bookings as a list or a full
calendar view, confirm or decline, and mark completed once the table's
been seated.

**Know your restaurant's real performance**
Deep Analytics shows your busiest day of the week, how your ratings are
trending, and what share of your bookings come from repeat guests — all
computed automatically from your own data.

**Team access, done right**
Invite staff as Managers (full access) or Menu Editors (menu, gallery, and
reviews only). Every staff member gets their own login, and if someone
forgets their password, they can reset it themselves — no need to come to
the owner.

**Stay in the loop**
Get platform announcements directly in your Inbox, reply if a response is
needed, and get push notifications the moment a new review or booking comes
in.

**Built for Iraq, in Arabic and English**
The entire app works in both languages, switch anytime. Every Iraqi
province and district is built in, so your location is always set up
correctly from day one.

New restaurants start under review and go live once approved — built to
keep the platform trustworthy for every customer who finds you on it.
